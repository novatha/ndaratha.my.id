Academic Profile

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the University of Bengkulu. My work focuses on the intersection of electrical power systems and computational intelligence. I hold a Ph.D. from IIT Roorkee, an M.Sc. from TU Delft, and an S.T. from Universitas Sriwijaya.

My primary research objective is to enhance the resilience and stability of power grids. I specialize in developing automated systems for contingency ranking, voltage security, and the strategic integration of renewable energy sources.
