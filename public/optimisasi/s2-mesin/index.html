Silabus S2 Teknik Mesin

Kurikulum Khusus Optimisasi Rekayasa Mesin & Sistem Struktur (Studi Kasus S2 UNIB).

Modul Bab 1

Bab 1: Pendahuluan & Pemodelan Rekayasa Mesin

Formulasi optimisasi bejana tekan (pressure vessel), fungsi tujuan massa material, dan kendala tegangan izin.

Modul Bab 2

Bab 2: Landasan Matematis & Konveksitas Himpunan

Ruang vektor, matriks Hessian, fungsi cembung, dan pembuktian konveksitas daerah feasible.

Modul Bab 3

Bab 3: Bracketing Methods

Golden Section Search, Bisection, Fibonacci Search pada optimisasi ketebalan isolasi termal pipa uap.

Modul Bab 4

Bab 4: Local Descent & Line Search

Gradient Descent, Steepest Descent, Armijo line search pada parameter pembubutan poros engkol.

Modul Bab 5

Bab 5: First-Order Methods & Perceptron

Momentum, Nesterov, Conjugate Gradient, dan optimisasi bobot jaringan Perceptron artificial intelligence.

Modul Bab 6

Bab 6: Second-Order Methods (Newton vs BFGS)

Metode Newton murni vs Quasi-Newton (BFGS/DFP) pada tekuk kolom elastik FEA skala besar (10^4 DOF).

Modul Bab 7

Bab 7: Constrained Optimization & Augmented Lagrangian

KKT Conditions, Penalty & Barrier Functions, Augmented Lagrangian pada dimensi balok I-beam kantilever.

Modul Bab 8

Bab 8: Multi-Objective & Surrogate-Based Optimization

Pareto fronts, Weighted Sum, Kriging surrogate model pada desain aerodinamika/struktur.

Modul Bab 9

Bab 9: Robust & Reliability-Based Optimization

Uncertainty Quantification (UQ), Chance Constraints pada keandalan operasional mesin.
