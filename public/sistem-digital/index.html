Matriks 16 Pertemuan

Peta Kurikulum & Silabus Mingguan

Pengajaran berbasis Outcomes-Based Education (OBE) untuk S1 Teknik Informatika.

Minggu 1 Manual & Calc

Pengantar & Sistem Bilangan

Biner, Oktal, Heksadesimal & 2's Complement

Minggu 2 Problem Set

Kode Digital & Aritmatika Biner

BCD, Gray Code, ASCII, Parity Bit, & Overflow

Minggu 3 Logisim Evolution

Gerbang Logika & IC Digital

AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR & IC 74xx

Minggu 4 Logisim & Teori

Aljabar Boolean & De Morgan

Postulat Boolean, Minterm/Maxterm, & SOP/POS

Minggu 5 Logisim Workshop

Peta Karnaugh (K-Map 2-4 Variabel)

Grouping, Don't Care (X), & Universal NAND

Minggu 6 Logisim & Script

K-Map 5 Variabel & Quine-McCluskey

Overlay Maps & Algoritma Tabulasi Analitis

Minggu 7 Tinkercad Circuits

Rangkaian Aritmatika Kombinasional

Half/Full Adder, Subtractor, Ripple Carry, CLA

Minggu 8 Written Test (20%)

Ujian Tengah Semester (UTS)

Evaluasi Komprehensif Teori CPMK-1 & CPMK-2

Minggu 9 Tinkercad Circuits

Encoder, Decoder, & 7-Segment

Priority Encoder (74148), Decoder BCD 7447

Minggu 10 Logisim Evolution

Multiplexer (MUX) & Demultiplexer

MUX 2:1/4:1/8:1, DEMUX, Sintesis Logika

Minggu 11 Logisim Evolution

Flip-Flops & Pewaktuan

SR, D, JK, T Flip-Flop, Edge-Trigger, Timing

Minggu 12 Logisim Checkpoint 1

Registers & Register Geser

Shift Register SISO, SIPO, PISO, PIPO, Universal

Minggu 13 Logisim Workshop

Pencacah (Counter) Sinkron/Asinkron

Ripple Counter, Synchronous Up/Down, Modulo-N

Minggu 14 Logisim Checkpoint 2

Finite State Machine (FSM)

Model Mealy & Moore, State Diagram & Reduction

Minggu 15 Demo Logisim/Hardware

Arsitektur Memori & Presentasi Tubas

RAM/ROM, PLA/PAL, Demo Proyek Hardware

Minggu 16 Comprehensive (25%)

Ujian Akhir Semester (UAS)

Evaluasi Komprehensif CPMK-3, 4, & 5

Lihat Rincian Lengkap RPS & Capaian Pembelajaran (CPMK)

Rincian standar CPL-1 s.d CPL-4, CPMK-1 s.d CPMK-5, serta bobot evaluasi resmi.
